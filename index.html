Strategic Insights & Technical Findings

The Mathematical Death of Mid-Range

Analytics proved 3-pointers (1.05 points/shot) and rim finishes vastly outperform long 2s (0.78 points/shot).

Pace Cyclicality

Pace collapsed from 115 in the 1960s to a defensive trench in 1999 (88.9 pace) before rebounding to 100 in the modern era.

Positionless Basketball

Centers transitioned from low-post bruising to perimeter playmaking and 3-point floor spacing.

True Shooting Evolution

League-wide True Shooting % rose from 48% in the early 1960s to an all-time record 56.5% today.
